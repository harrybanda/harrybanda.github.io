Who Let the Flies In?

Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy covers Who Let the Flies In?, a mixed-reality game for Meta Quest, including its testing versions. I am Harry Banda, an individual developer based in Zambia, responsible for the game's use of your information. Contact me at contact@harrybanda.com about privacy, access, correction or deletion.

The game uses tracking information to let you play, stores progress and playtest records on your headset, and uses Meta account information for online rankings when those features are enabled.

Online rankings can show your Meta display name or username, score and rank to other players. Resetting progress does not delete historical playtest records or online leaderboard entries.

1. Information used to run the game

Tracking and mixed reality

The game processes hand, pinch, controller and headset tracking information to operate the chopsticks and jar, detect gestures, and position the interface and play area. This includes positions, tracking availability and pinch strength. These inputs are processed on the device. The game's progress and ordinary playtest files do not contain recordings of raw hand-joint or headset-position streams.

Meta's passthrough system displays your surroundings. The game does not read or record the raw camera feed, record your microphone, or collect GPS location. Meta's operating system processes sensor information separately to provide tracking and passthrough.

Progress and settings

The game stores your scores, virtual Buzz balance, upgrades, discovered flies, equipped gear, rewards, tutorial progress and preferences on the headset. These records let you continue playing between sessions. Buzz and upgrades are game progress, not payment-card information.

Local playtest and diagnostic records

The game writes local records to help investigate faults and improve gameplay balance. They include random session and round identifiers, dates and times, game version, settings and preferred hand, round duration, time when tracking is unavailable, scores, catches and misses, power use, jar-shot results, upgrades and discoveries. They do not include your Meta account ID, username, profile photo, camera footage or audio.

Local testing builds can also record limited tracking-state, pinch-strength and catch-distance diagnostics. These extra diagnostic files rotate within a size limit. Ordinary playtest history has no automatic expiry.

The game's playtest recorder does not automatically upload these files. If you agree to provide logs for a playtest or support request, I keep those player logs on my own computer and do not send them to AI services or cloud storage. Contact me first so we can arrange an appropriate local transfer; please do not attach player logs to an email.

2. Meta account information and rankings

When online rankings are enabled in your version, the game uses Meta's platform services to check that your account can access the game, identify you by your app-scoped Meta User ID, and obtain your display name or username. It reads leaderboard entries and uses Meta's Friends filter to show eligible friends' rankings. It does not download a separate copy of your full friends list or display profile photos.

Eligible completed Arcade rounds automatically submit the final score, including the jar bonus, to Meta's leaderboard service. Meta associates that score with your account and retains leaderboard entries. Your displayed name, score and rank may be visible to other players in global and friends rankings and, if enabled, through Meta's own leaderboard surfaces. Opening the Friends view does not make your global entry private.

The game temporarily keeps account information and retrieved rankings in session memory. It does not store a separate local account database or upload historical saved personal bests. Builds marked LOCAL TEST do not submit public scores. Playing without a network connection prevents this game's online score submissions, but simply closing the Rankings screen does not disable them.

Online features may be unavailable in earlier or testing versions. This policy describes their use when enabled; it does not mean every version includes all ranking periods.

When Daily and Weekly rankings are enabled, the game requests the current leaderboard names and UTC periods from a small service hosted by Cloudflare. The game sends no Meta user ID, profile, score, access token or gameplay log to this service. It does not add query parameters or an account identifier to the request.

3. Platforms, services and other recipients

  • Meta provides Quest, account and entitlement services, tracking, passthrough and leaderboards. Its own processing is described in Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Unity provides the game engine. Earlier builds may have Unity's automatic Diagnostics enabled. That service can process installation identifiers and technical device, software, crash and error information to diagnose stability problems. The next release will disable automatic Unity Diagnostics. The game does not enable Unity Ads or Unity Analytics. See Unity's game-player privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts the Daily/Weekly period service. It processes ordinary connection information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect requests. The game\'s period service does not log request data, and its request logging and tracing are disabled. This does not mean that Cloudflare retains no operational network data. Scores and player profiles continue to be handled by Meta. See Cloudflare\'s Privacy Policy.
  • Other players can see the leaderboard information described above. They do not receive your local progress files or playtest logs.
  • Email services deliver and store messages you send to the privacy/support address. I use your email address and message to respond and handle your request. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this policy page. Visiting it sends ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to GitHub. This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, forms or tracking cookies added by me. See GitHub's privacy statement.

I do not sell or rent your player information, use it for targeted advertising, or use hand or headset data to identify you biometrically. Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law. A new service or a material change in how information is used will be reflected in this policy before that new use begins.

4. Purposes and retention

I process information to provide gameplay, preserve progress, operate rankings, investigate problems, improve gameplay, respond to requests and meet legal obligations. Where applicable, this processing is based on providing the service you request, legitimate interests in maintaining a functioning game, or your consent where required.

  • Live tracking inputs and cached account/ranking information are used during play or the current session rather than written to the progress or playtest files.
  • Local progress remains until reset or removal of the app's local data. Local playtest records remain until their files or the app's local data are removed; Reset Progress does not remove them.
  • Online scores remain with Meta for the leaderboard's operation until removed through the applicable service or a deletion request. Resetting or uninstalling the game does not itself delete them.
  • Player logs provided to me and support correspondence are kept only as long as needed for the playtest, support issue or request, or a legal obligation. When no longer needed, I delete them or remove information that can relate them to a person or device.

Meta, Unity, Cloudflare and the services that deliver email or host this page may process information in countries other than yours under their applicable terms, privacy policies and safeguards.

5. Your choices and deletion requests

Reset Progress in the game's settings resets gameplay progress and preferences. It does not erase historical playtest files, copies you previously shared, or Meta leaderboard entries. Removing local app data also removes local progress and logs; any platform backup or separately held copy needs to be handled separately.

For access, correction or deletion, email contact@harrybanda.com with the subject Who Let the Flies In privacy request. Describe the information concerned. For rankings, your Meta username and approximate score/date can help locate the entry. For local files, I can explain how to remove them. Never send your password, access token or payment details.

I may ask for limited information to verify that a request relates to you. I will handle requests without undue delay and within applicable legal time limits, including coordinating removal of the game's leaderboard records or relevant diagnostics with the platform where necessary. Removing a leaderboard entry does not prevent a later eligible online round from creating a new one. Requests about your entire Meta account should also be made through Meta's account controls.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, obtain a portable copy of information, withdraw consent where relied on, or complain to your local data-protection authority.

6. Younger players

Players must meet the age and account requirements shown in the game's Meta listing and follow Meta's rules for their account type. If you are a parent or guardian and believe information from a child has been handled improperly, contact me so I can investigate and arrange deletion where appropriate.

7. Security and changes

I limit access to information used for support and testing and use the platform's account and service security controls. Local files rely on the headset's and computer's access protections; they are not encrypted by a separate encryption system built into this game. No system can guarantee absolute security.

I will publish changes at this address and update the date above. Material changes will be explained before the new processing begins, with additional notice or consent where required.
